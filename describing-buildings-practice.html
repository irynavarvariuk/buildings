Describing buildings and places

Six exercises that take the lesson vocabulary from memory retrieval to free production. Work through them in order: the support gets lighter each time.

1 Recall 2 Gaps with hints 3 Upgrade 4 Small words 5 Modifiers 6 Create

1. Recall the word

Controlled practice. Focus: memory retrieval of all target vocabulary.

Aim: retrieve the English form from its meaning, with no sentence and no help, so the word has to come from memory.

Read the Ukrainian prompt and type the English word or expression. Press Enter or Check. Use Hint only after a real attempt. Patterns with dots stand for words you can choose.

2. Fill the gap, with first letters

Controlled practice. Focus: using the word in context.

Aim: choose the right word because the sentence needs it, not because you remember a list. The first letters keep the task manageable.

Type the rest of the word, or the whole word. Hyphens and capital letters do not matter.

    3. Upgrade the sentence

    Semi-controlled practice. Focus: sentence-level production.

    Aim: say the same idea more precisely. You choose the structure, and the lesson vocabulary has to carry the meaning.

    Rewrite each plain sentence in your own words. Use at least two of the units shown. They light up when you use them. Then compare with the model: yours can be different and still correct.

    4. The small words that hold collocations together

    Controlled practice. Focus: accuracy of prepositions, articles and determiners.

    Aim: notice the tiny words inside collocations. Learners often know the content words and still lose marks on of, an, its and any.

    Type the one missing word. After checking, the full collocation appears under the sentence.

      5. Choose the modifier

      Controlled practice. Focus: modifiers and intensifiers.

      Aim: see that modifiers are not interchangeable. Extreme adjectives such as hideous reject very, overly adds criticism, and somewhat softens.

      Click the option that fits. A short explanation appears after each answer.

      6. Describe a place

      Freer practice. Focus: extended production and self-assessment.

      Aim: use the vocabulary to say something you actually think. The counter shows how much of the lesson you have activated.

      Choose a topic and write 5 to 7 sentences: what it looks like, how it feels, and your opinion. Try to light up at least 10 units.
